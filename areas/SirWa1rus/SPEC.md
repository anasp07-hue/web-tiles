# DVD Screensaver

The DVD logo bounces around a black screen, like the old DVD player screensaver.

- The logo moves diagonally and bounces off the edges of the window.
- Each time it hits an edge, the logo changes to a new color.
- When it hits a corner exactly, the screen flashes in the logo's color and the
  "Corner hits" count at the top left goes up by one.
- Space pauses and resumes the logo.
- The Up and Down arrow keys make the logo faster or slower (speeds 1 to 12, starting at 3).
- A click changes the logo's color.
- The screen fills the whole window and follows its size; the logo is one fifth of the
  window's width.
- The logo image is `dvd-logo.png`, in this folder.
