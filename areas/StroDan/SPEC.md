# Firefly Chase

A swarm of fireflies chases the pointer across a night meadow.

- 80 fireflies at the start, at random positions. Each blinks on its own cycle and circles
  the swarm's target at its own distance and speed.
- Move the mouse (or drag a finger) and the swarm follows it.
- If the pointer leaves the window or stays still for 4 seconds, the swarm wanders in a slow
  loop by itself, which is what a gallery tile shows.
- Click or tap to scatter the fireflies within 350 pixels; they then regroup.
- Space switches between **follow** and **flee**. In flee mode the fireflies spread across the
  meadow and dodge the pointer.
- The Up and Down arrow keys add or remove 10 fireflies, from 10 up to 300.
- The current count and mode are shown at the bottom right.
- The meadow fills the whole window and follows its size. Keyboard input works after clicking
  inside the area.
- With the system's reduced motion setting on, the fireflies glow steadily instead of blinking.
