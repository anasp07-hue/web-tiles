# Christmas Night

A snowy night with a Christmas tree. Snow falls, the tree's lights twinkle, and snow slowly
piles up on the ground.

- Snowflakes of different sizes fall and sway from side to side; bigger flakes fall faster.
  The number of flakes depends on the window size.
- When a flake reaches the ground it adds a little to the snow there, so drifts build up over
  time (up to 60 pixels).
- The tree stands in the middle, with 40 colored lights that each brighten and dim on their
  own rhythm, and a glowing star on top. A moon glows in the upper right.
- A click adds a burst of 40 snowflakes around the pointer.
- Space turns the tree lights and the star on and off.
- The scene fills the whole window and follows its size. Resizing starts the snow over.
